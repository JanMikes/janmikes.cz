Don’t be afraid to move on

Nebát se posunout se dále. A talk about getting from point A to point B: why I went looking for a new job, what I wanted, what the market actually offered, and how hard it is to pick. I gave it at PHPlive in Ostrava and repeated it the next day, 28 February, in Žilina.

This was a personal story more than a technical talk, and the slides are mostly pictures. Most of it was said out loud, so if you want the full story, watch the video linked below. Here is the outline.

Could you be happier?

I opened with a question to the room: who thinks they could be happier? Then a number. A person spends on average ten years of their life at work. Who would want to be unhappy for ten years voluntarily?

Why I was looking

Why did I look for a new job? The slides answered with two pictures: an empty wallet, then someone buried in banknotes. Money was clearly part of it.

Set your criteria first

Before talking to anyone, write down what you want. These were mine:

  • Starting in February
  • Part-time
  • Remote work and home office possible
  • Good pay
  • In Prague
  • A free, open culture
  • Learning and education
  • Working on one project

On the next slide the last one was crossed out. Working on one project was the criterion I gave up on along the way.

Expectations vs reality

In an ideal world, the more you ask for, the more companies want to work with you. In reality it’s the opposite: interest is at its peak at the lowest number on the chart, 45K, and drops to zero at 120K. The two lines cross somewhere around 85K.

Huge demand

And still, the demand for developers was huge. Offers came from everywhere: LinkedIn, StartupJobs, techloop.io, Jobs.cz, and the Péhápkaři community with its Slack. The slide after that was a planet blowing up.

Which one do I pick?

So many offers that choosing becomes the hard part. The slide for this was a line of beauty-pageant contestants: they all look great, which one is the right one?

Is it the right one? In my case the answer on the slide was appload digital.

Don’t be afraid of it

The closing slide was the familiar drawing of two circles: a small one labelled “your comfort zone”, and a big one, outside it, labelled “where the magic happens”. Changing jobs is scary. Don’t be afraid of it.
