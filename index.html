How I work

  • Legacy code is where I’m most useful. I genuinely like technical debt. Taming large systems that outgrew the assumptions they were built on is a rare skill, and it’s the one I’m best at.
  • Deploys should take minutes. MySpeedPuzzling goes from push to production in about two and a half minutes. Every change passes PHPStan and a test suite sharded three ways.
  • Own the infrastructure. Self-hosted on Hetzner, open-source components wherever possible. The chatbot from my RAG talk answers around 100 conversations a day for about $3 a month.
  • Public by default. Whatever can be open source, is. That includes the entire MySpeedPuzzling codebase.
  • Agents are on the team now. I was a sceptic. Today Claude Code runs for days on my projects, and I speak about how to make that work.
